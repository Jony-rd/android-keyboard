pboard language packs (en.lm, en_GB.lmo, en_US.lmo, en.nnlm)
=============================================================

These files contain statistics (pruned, quantised n-gram probabilities, word frequencies and spelling-variant
preferences; for en.nnlm, the int8 weights of a small LSTM language model trained on the same text) derived
from the corpora listed below. They are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Build pipeline and full audit: tools/lm/ and docs/data-licenses.md in the pboard source repository.
